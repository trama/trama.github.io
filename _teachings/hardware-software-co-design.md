## Program

Master's Degree in Electronics Engineering, 1st Year - 1st Semester.

## Description

Module responsibility for a 2 ECTS section in the measurements area (IMIS-01/B). The teaching activity introduces simulation of electronic circuits with SPICE, with emphasis on filtering stages for signal conditioning.

## Academic Years

Active from A.Y. 2024/2025 to present.
