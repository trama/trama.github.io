## Program

Master's Degree in Electronics Engineering, 1st Year - 2nd Semester.

## Description

The course includes lectures and laboratory activities focused on industrial measurement systems. The laboratory includes an introduction to LabVIEW and an independent project for the design and implementation of a measurement system based on the techniques and sensors presented in class.

## Academic Years

Active from A.Y. 2020/2021 to present.
